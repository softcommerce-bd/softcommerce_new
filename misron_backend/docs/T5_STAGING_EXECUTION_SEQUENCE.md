## Rollback decision tree
- Before `up` (steps 5-8 fail): restore the window backup; nothing business happened.
- After `up`: no restore (business happened). Fix forward; the index migration alone can be rolled back with its `down()`; the engine conversion is one-way in practice (reverting to MyISAM is mechanically possible but removes the guarantees and re-disables retries/purchasing).
- Never roll the scheduler host back to a release older than `25aec82` (re-enables the old auto-deposit job).

## Statement
Completing this sequence would give **staging evidence**. It would not by itself be staging acceptance, production acceptance or T-5 closure; those remain the owner's written decisions (E11) after the accountant's and DBA's items are returned.
